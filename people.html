Dr. Chetan Prakash Sharma

Principal Investigator, LITE Laboratory (research initiative)
Postdoctoral Researcher, Chemical & Materials Engineering, New Jersey Institute of Technology

Chetan develops laser-induced graphene materials and devices for antimicrobial surfaces, water-treatment membranes, air filters and electrochemical sensors. During his PhD at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev he co-developed a low-voltage, virus- and bacteria-killing graphene air filter that was later commercialized by LIGC Applications. At NJIT he works on impedimetric microfluidic sensors and real-time membrane fouling monitoring.

His work has been featured in The Engineer and The Jerusalem Post.
